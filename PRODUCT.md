# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Sourcegraph blog readers, developers, engineering leaders, and researchers who want to understand what repository-scale indexed history can reveal about AI-assisted development.

## Product Purpose

Turn the frozen investigation artifacts into an evidence-led blog post. The post should teach readers something specific about how AI adoption appears in repository history, show what the Sourcegraph index made observable, and make the limits of each claim easy to inspect.

## Brand Personality

Curious, technical, and candid. The writing can have an opinion, but the charts and captions must distinguish observations from attribution.

## Anti-references

- A correction memo organized around an earlier misleading result.
- A generic research dashboard made from interchangeable metric cards.
- A Sourcegraph-colored shell around charts that do not follow Sourcegraph's visual language.
- A methodology dump that asks the reader to find the interesting result.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the finding, then show the evidence that supports it.
2. Use repository history as the story, not as background plumbing.
3. Put denominators and claim boundaries next to the mark they qualify.
4. Prefer direct labels and comparisons over legends and decorative diagrams.
5. Make every artifact traceable without making the audit trail the headline.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA, preserve meaning without color, expose chart values as text on small screens, support reduced motion, and keep the standalone HTML usable without scripts or network access.
